WHO I AM

The person behind GoldenGetaways.

I'm the person behind GoldenGetaways, and I started it because I wanted to build something that made finding holidays feel simpler and more personal.

Searching for a holiday can quickly become overwhelming. There are hundreds of hotels, different websites, changing prices and endless options to compare.

I wanted GoldenGetaways to give people somewhere they could come for inspiration, useful holiday recommendations and, as a member, personalised help finding something that actually suits them.

Behind the brand

WHY I STARTED GOLDENGETAWAYS

Holidays should feel exciting, not complicated.

I started GoldenGetaways because I could see how much time people spend scrolling through holiday websites, comparing prices and trying to work out which option is actually worth booking.

I wanted to create something different: a place where people could discover handpicked getaway inspiration without having to search through thousands of options themselves.

The goal was never just to show people holidays. I wanted members to be able to tell us what they're looking for — their budget, preferred airport, destination and travel style — and have help narrowing everything down.

THE IDEA

A travel membership that feels genuinely useful.

GoldenGetaways is built around making holiday discovery more enjoyable. That means handpicked holiday inspiration, personalised searches and support when members need help.

I also wanted GoldenGetaways to feel like a community rather than another faceless website. Members should know there are real people behind the service who actually care about making it better.

As GoldenGetaways grows, the aim is simple: keep improving what members get, keep finding better ways to help people discover holidays and keep the service personal.

WHAT GOLDENGETAWAYS STANDS FOR

Built around three simple ideas.

Everything I want GoldenGetaways to become comes back to making the experience easier, more personal and genuinely useful for members.
